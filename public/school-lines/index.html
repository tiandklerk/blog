School Lines

School systems drawn as metro lines. Each station is a stage of schooling, placed at the age pupils usually reach it. Branches show the routes pupils can take, and dotted lines show transfers between them. Switch the stations to show fares (fees) or how many people get that far. Pick a country, or compare countries side by side by age.

Explore a country Compare by age

Ages are typical starting ages; individual pupils vary with birthdays, repeating or skipping years, and regional rules. Stage levels follow the ISCED 2011 scale. Fares are typical fees at public providers and exclude living costs. Attainment, PPP and GDP figures come from UNESCO-UIS and the World Bank. Every country lists its sources. This is not official guidance; check with the relevant school or authority for decisions.
